# Concordante Lab — Research Overview
**Xorch**

Concordante Lab is an independent research program exploring mathematical structures, symmetry, discrete systems, computational verification, and formal questions about information and change.

Its central methodological question is: *Which minimal relational structure explains the present state while preserving the distinctions that matter?*

## Main directions

**Geometry and symmetry.** Orbits, reflections, incidence patterns, and root systems provide precise mathematical settings for structural questions.

**Discrete mathematics.** Graphs, finite configurations, exact paths, and combinatorial constraints offer testable problems.

**Dynamical systems and information.** Formal states, transitions, histories, and invariants help clarify what can be reconstructed or predicted.

**Reproducible computation.** Programs, certificates, independent checks, and adversarial cases are recorded according to their actual scope.

## Evidence and status

Concordante Lab distinguishes a proved theorem from a finite computational verification, a working hypothesis, or a conceptual exploration. Manuscripts in preparation and published research articles retain their respective editorial status.

This repository includes curated introductory texts and scientific releases covering geometry, root systems, couplings, networks, number theory, physical models, and open questions, each with a stated scope. Private working originals and personal or administrative data remain separate. See the [scientific archive](archivo-cientifico/README.md) for evidence, limitations and editorial policy.
